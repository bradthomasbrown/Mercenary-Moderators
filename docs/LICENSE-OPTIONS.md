# License decision

The owner selected **PolyForm Shield 1.0.0** on 2026-09-11 and authorized public repository visibility. The adopted text is in [LICENSE](../LICENSE), with scope and required notices in [NOTICE](../NOTICE). [Third-party exceptions](../THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md) retain their own licenses.

Shield allows inspection, modification and distribution for permitted purposes while restricting providing competing products. Its competition definition includes free substitutes. It is a source-available license, not an OSI open-source license.

[CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md) welcomes independent feature work and pull requests without prior approval or a separate CLA. Intentionally submitted contributions use the license applicable to the contributed material; contributors retain copyright and maintainers decide what to merge. Merging does not automatically grant broader relicensing rights.

## Scope and limits

Shield covers competition with the software and products the licensor or affiliates provide using it. Its New Products clause protects previously permitted uses in specified circumstances. NOTICE identifies browser-based post tagging, filtering, and tag-sharing markets as the licensor's line of business for the Discontinued Products clause. The complete license controls.

The license does not compel all private improvements upstream, prohibit independently written competitors, replace third-party rights, or create copyright in material without sufficient protectable authorship. The WebKit-derived button stylesheet remains LGPL-2.0-or-later.

## Alternatives considered

PolyForm Strict was rejected because its general modification restriction obstructs independent development and contributions. PolyForm Noncommercial does not address free competing forks. PolyForm Perimeter offers a narrower competition restriction. TypeScript's Apache 2.0 license permits competing forks; its separate CLA process was not adopted here.
